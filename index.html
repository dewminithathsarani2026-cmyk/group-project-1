 📝Today Our School
Established in 1835, this government boys’ school is renowned for academic excellence, sports, and leadership development, following the national curriculum.
NovaBridge College Colombo, originally Colombo Academy (founded 1835 by Rev. Joseph Marsh), became “Royal” in 1881 by Queen Victoria’s decree. It is Sri Lanka’s oldest and most prestigious public boys’ school, offering education free of charge since 1931.
